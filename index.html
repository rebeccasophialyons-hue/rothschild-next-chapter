The concept

New wealth, introduced to old money.

A private, three-chapter event series that brings Israel's most successful founders into the world of art, ritual and taste, and makes Edmond de Rothschild the house that opened the door.

01 · The opportunity

They have the capital. Not yet the circle.

A generation of Israeli founders has created significant wealth in a remarkably short time. Few have yet formed a lasting relationship with a heritage private bank. The first house to earn their trust is likely to keep it.

  • Liquidity is arriving earlierSecondaries and exits now realise founder wealth years before a traditional IPO.
  • Relationships are still unformedAt this stage, loyalties are open. In five years, they will not be.
  • Competitors lead with productMost banks approach founders with a pitch. Very few arrive with an invitation.
  • Entry, not accessWealth buys access to almost anything. It does not buy a place in the world that has always signalled established wealth.
  • Cultural fluencyArt, collecting, fine watchmaking and wine: the codes of old money, learned from those who keep them.
  • A trusted counterpartAn adviser who understands both the business they built and the family they are building.
The approach

We don't pitch. We host.

A cold introductionA personal invitation
A product presentationA cultural experience

A prospect listA relationship for generations
