The chapters

Three invitations into our world.

Art, time and wine. Three evenings in Israel, each one opening a different door into the world of Edmond de Rothschild.

i
Chapter I · Tel Aviv

The Founders’ Table.

An after-hours evening with a curator and three emerging Israeli artists, followed by a candlelit dinner inside the exhibition.

Hosted at the Edmond de Rothschild Center, Tel Aviv

ii
Chapter II · Swiss heritage

The Art of Time.

A Swiss watch specialist and a collector introduce the codes of fine watchmaking, from craft and movement to collectability.

Hosted in a private salon, Tel Aviv or Jaffa

iii
Chapter III · Rothschild wine

Behind the Label.

Rooted in the story of Château Clarke and the Edmond de Rothschild wine portfolio. Guests learn what good wine tastes like, how to understand it and how to remember it.

Hosted as a private tasting in Israel
